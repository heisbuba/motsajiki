Help & Info

Quick answers on how Motsa Jiki works, plus the legal stuffs to know the boundaries.

FAQ

Do I need an account to use Motsa Jiki?

No. Every log, target, and badge is stored right on your device — there's no sign-up and no login.

How do Local Folder and Google Drive sync work?

Both are optional and off by default. Local Folder saves a copy of your data to a folder you choose on this device, using your browser's file access permission. Google Drive saves a single workout.json file to your My Drive — it never reads or touches any of your other Drive files.

Does Motsa Jiki work offline?

Yes. It's built as an installable, offline-first app — logging workouts, tracking goals, and viewing badges all work with no connection. A network is only needed the moment you choose to sync to cloud.

How do I export my data?

Use the export option on the Data tab to save your logs as a CSV file, or generate a shareable PNG summary of your stats and proudly flex it.

How do I disconnect sync or remove my data?

Tap the sync icon in the header to disconnect Local Folder or Google Drive at any time. Clearing this site's data from your browser removes everything Motsa Jiki has stored locally.
